Four ways Commandery could look

Nine of the screens you'd use most, in four directions, on a phone and on a laptop. The screens, the numbers and the way areas are laid out are the same in all four. Only the look changes. The cards, prices, scans and import are yours, from your Mythic Tools export. The decks' contents and the boxes other than "Bulk list" are examples.

The four directions

My pick: D, with B's discipline

D is the only direction that is calm and also feels like yours. Rose paper, plum ink and cards in sleeves read like a binder on a kitchen table, which is where you build. It carries the purple and pink you like without shouting, and it doesn't look like every other web app.

A is the safest, but it's the look of a thousand Tailwind apps, which the brief asked to avoid. C is the most striking, but its gradients and dark surfaces fight "calm", and it only works at night. B is the calmest of all, but it drops the card art and the flair that make Magic fun to sort.

From B I'd keep three rules in D: one primary button per screen, text-first rows when you're searching the collection (art turns on with one tap), and hairlines instead of boxes inside lists. I'd also give D an evening palette in deep plum, because scanning often happens at night.

Name and logo

A commandery was the house and lands a commander kept. Here it's where your commanders and their cards live: boxes, binders and decks. The mark is three cards standing together, with the commander in the middle standing tallest. Other names I'd consider: Brewbox (brewing decks out of boxes) and Kitchen Table (where Commander is played). The name and the direction are separate choices.
